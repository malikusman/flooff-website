Meet Duke, the real Pet Butler

He is large, black and spectacularly goofy.

While we call him our dumb goofy boy, Duke is utterly devoted to looking after dogs smaller than himself.

Pet Butler is named after him because it does exactly what Duke does — watches out for pets who need someone in their corner.

Pets First. Everything else follows.
Pets should have the best possible life. The people who love them shouldn't have to fight the system to get the best.

We've watched that fight up close. Our team runs and supports animal welfare programmes across the Emirates. We work alongside dog and cat rescue organisations. We've seen pet owners surrender their pets and turn a blind eye when the pet has severe health issues. We've watched first-time pet parents give up on adopting because the logistics looked impossible from the outside.

Every pet parent knows the exact panic of a Friday night when the vet doesn't pick up and the groomer's booked out for a fortnight. The UAE isn't short of pet care. It's short of access to it. People aren't short on choice. They're worn out from evaluating what's best suited.

So we stopped adding names to a directory and built something that decides with you.

The Flooff Standard

Four gates, before we onboard any provider — and nobody can pay to skip one.

( 01 )

Legal standing

Valid UAE trade licence.

( 02 )

Professional credentials

Veterinary registration, grooming certification and handling qualifications, confirmed at source.

( 03 )

Operational history

Facility standards and years in operation.

( 04 )

Ongoing performance

Every booking leaves a service record. We work with our providers, and those falling below standard come out of the network.

There's no paid tier, no promoted placement and no advertising product. If that ever changes, it will be said here first.

Two questions people ask

Is Pet Butler really free?

Yes. You pay the provider's own price, through us — no booking fee, no service charge, nothing added on top. We earn from partnerships with the providers in our network, not from you.

So does he recommend whoever pays you most?

No. No provider can pay to rank — no promoted placement, no featured tier, at any price. He recommends on fit, availability, and how that provider has actually performed for other pets.
